THE ORIGIN

The Human Time System (HTS) was created with a single, utilitarian purpose: to make keeping track of the calendar year easier for future generations.

The Premise

The premise is simple: 5 numbers and a single letter.

1 Supercycle
A Epoch
40000 HTS Year

The leading number (1) represents the Supercycle. This number can be infinitely large if needed, ensuring the system never "runs out" of time.

Current Status

We are currently in Epoch A. This epoch will not end for another 9,997 years.

When that time comes, the year 49999 will simply roll over to 00000, and the letter will change to B. No computer crashes, no confusion, just a simple transition.

Usability

Relative to a human lifetime, the Supercycle and Epoch are virtually static. They are almost never used in day-to-day conversation, much like we rarely reference the geological era we currently inhabit.

This design ensures the system is infinitely usable, yet remains limited in difficulty for the average user.
