About HTS

THE ORIGIN

HTS was created to simplify the calendar for future generations. It uses a stable 5-digit year to provide a timeline that is easy to use and lasts for tens of billions of years.

DECIMAL PRECISION

By utilizing a base-10 epoch system, HTS eliminates the irregular "leap year" logic found in traditional Gregorian systems.

UNIVERSAL ADOPTION

Designed for interplanetary synchronization, HTS provides a unified rhythmic anchor for a multi-planetary human civilization.

ECOSYSTEM

Integrating biological and digital rhythms into a single, cohesive framework of time perception.

THE HUMAN TIME SYSTEM FOUNDATION

A future entity responsible for worldwide governance 98,000 years from now. Their mission: To ensure the world never exceeds 5-digit years, preventing the tedium of over-extended dates by cycling Epochs instead of adding digits.
